Meter Reader app screens

The reader's full path from sign-in to the next meter, designed for field staff who read little and use phones mostly for calls and messages. Sample data from zones 597 and 598.

  • One big blue button per screenThe solid navy button is always the next step. Everything else is outlined and secondary.
  • Pictures and colour before wordsGreen drop = irrigation, brown pipe = sewerage. Green tick = done, amber = being checked, purple cloud = waiting to send, red = read again.
  • Speaker button on every screenReads the instruction aloud in simple English (Android text-to-speech), for readers who find reading hard.
  • Numbers look like the meterOdometer-style digit boxes, one per wheel, with the meter's own photo above while typing.
  • Photo first, then the numberThe reader copies the number from their own photo, so they are not reading a wet or dark meter twice.
  • The app finds the next meter"Start" opens the next unread meter in route order. Search by a few digits or by voice when a reader is sent somewhere specific.
  • Dubai Investments Park coloursLogo navy for actions, logo taupe for the brand and sewerage meters. English only, short everyday words.
  • No typing, no keyboardBig in-app keypad, picture tiles for status and reasons. Remarks can be spoken (voice-to-text).

Field Inspection: proposed screens

An inspector visits a planned property, checks each unit against the sub-tenant on record, records what they find (Subleased, Disputed, Rejected, Pending and two proposed statuses), counts the people seen, and takes photos of anything suspect. The screens follow the same rules as the meter-reading app: one navy button per screen, a read-aloud line on each, status shown by icon and words, and everything works without signal.

1. What the two views give us, and what is missing

From vw_MR_InspectionPlan and vw_MR_InspectionUnit as shown in SSMS on 2026-10-05. Items marked High need fixing before the app can be built on these views.

High Unit view stops at 1,000 rows

The MRDB view is SELECT TOP (1000) … (copied from SSMS's "Select Top 1000"). SSMS showed exactly 1,000 rows, so units beyond that are silently missing and some properties would look half-empty. Remove TOP (1000).

High Property codes may not match

Plan rows use codes like 597-581; some unit rows use 597-4900(Bldg17) or STR-597-4899BldgNo…. Units whose code is not in the plan never appear on any visit. Check before building: SELECT DISTINCT u.PropertyCode FROM vw_MR_InspectionUnit u WHERE NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM vw_MR_InspectionPlan p WHERE p.PropertyCode = u.PropertyCode).

High Units have no tenant

The plan is per property and tenant (TenantCode), but units only carry PropertyCode. If a property has two tenants, the app cannot tell which units belong to which visit. Add TenantCode to the unit view, or confirm one tenant per property per period.

Medium "All in order" is not a status

The list is Rejected, Subleased, Pending, Disputed: all are problems or "not done". Most units will be fine, so the inspector needs a quick As recorded (occupant matches). Proposed also: Vacant, for an active unit found empty. Pending then means "could not check" (locked, no access), with a reason.

Medium Who does which visit

The plan has no inspector and no zone. Proposed, like meter reading: plans are shared, narrowed by zone (from vw_MR_Property), and a visit is "taken" by the first inspector who starts it. Inspectors need a role: a Role column in vw_MR_Reader (Reader / Inspector / both) or a separate inspector view.

Medium Status meanings to confirm

Suggested wording (shown to inspectors, see section 3): what makes a unit Rejected versus Disputed? Should Subleased record the actual occupant's company name? (Proposed: yes, required.)

Low Category has empty levels

Commercial> > appears for some units. The app shows the last non-empty level ("Commercial") and the full path on the unit sheet.

Low Inactive units

Many properties have inactive units (598-1187: 2 active, 42 inactive). Proposed: inactive units are listed under "Inactive on record", collapsed; finding one occupied is the most important result, so it can be marked like any other.

Low No unit history

The inspector cannot see last period's result. Once results are stored (section 4), the unit sheet shows "Last inspection: Subleased, 2026-08" from our own tables.

4. How it fits the system

Phone

  • Same app, second tile; the inspector role decides what shows.
  • Plan and units saved on the phone for the week (encrypted, like the meter list).
  • Results and photos in the same encrypted queue, sent with "Send now / Later".

API (through zApps)

  • GET /api/v1/inspections/plan?period=&zone=, GET …/{property}/units
  • POST /api/v1/inspections (one visit with its unit results, safe to retry), photos by PUT as for readings.
  • The gateway's fixed list gets these routes; nothing else opens.

Database (MRDB, schema mr)

  • mr.InspectionVisit: property, tenant, period, inspector, phone, start/end time, location, person met.
  • mr.InspectionUnitResult: visit, UnitId (or new unit), result, people seen, occupant name, reasons, note.
  • Photos in the existing image store. The PMS views are only read, never written.

Office

  • Report per period: flagged units by property and zone, with photos.
  • Pending units and late plans.
  • Later, if wanted: send results back to PMS, insert-only, like the meter-reading copy.

Before building: remove TOP (1000) from vw_MR_InspectionUnit, confirm how unit property codes match the plan, add TenantCode to the units (or confirm one tenant per property), confirm the six results and their meanings, and decide how inspectors are identified (role in vw_MR_Reader or a new view).

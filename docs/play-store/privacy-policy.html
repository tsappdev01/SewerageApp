DIP Field Service – Privacy Policy

Effective date: <date> · Dubai Investments Park (<legal entity name>)

DIP Field Service ("the app") is a work app used by staff and contractors of Dubai Investments Park ("DIP", "we") to read sewerage and irrigation meters and to inspect units. It is not for the public: it works only on phones registered by DIP. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what we do with it.

What the app collects

DataWhenWhy
Your DIP reader account (email) and nameWhen the phone is set up for youTo know who recorded each reading and visit
Meter readings, meter condition, notes, and the tenant you confirmed on siteWhen you record a meterBilling of sewerage and irrigation use
Photos of meters and unitsWhen you take them in the appEvidence of the reading or of what was found
Inspection results, the occupant's name and number of people seen if you enter them, and an optional signatureDuring a field inspectionChecking that units are used as recorded
Precise location (GPS)Only while you record a field inspection visit, never in the backgroundShowing the visit was made at the property
A device ID and key made by the app, the phone model, Android/iOS version and app versionWhen the phone is registeredAllowing only registered phones to send data, and support
Network address and time of each requestWhen the app talks to DIP's serverSecurity and audit logs

The app does not read your contacts, messages, calls, files, calendar or other apps, has no advertising, and uses no analytics or tracking services. Voice search uses the phone's own speech service; the app receives only the text.

How it is sent and stored

Data is sent only to DIP's own servers, over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Work waiting to be sent, and the recent work shown in the app, is kept on the phone in the app's own encrypted storage. The app can ask for the phone's lock before it opens.

Sharing

We do not sell data or share it with third parties for their own use. It is used inside DIP for meter billing, inspections, and audit. It may be disclosed where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Readings, photos and inspection records are kept as long as DIP needs them for billing, tenancy and audit purposes and as required by UAE law: <retention period>. Security logs are kept for <period>.

Your choices and rights

Camera and location can be refused in the phone's settings, but then readings or inspections cannot be completed. To ask what data we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted where the law allows, contact <DIP IT / data protection contact, email>. When you stop using the app, your supervisor withdraws the phone's registration and the app's data can be removed by uninstalling it.

Children

The app is for adult staff only and is not meant for children.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes what it collects, and change the date above.

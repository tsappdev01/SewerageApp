## Full description (4000)

DIP Field Service is the work app for Dubai Investments Park's sewerage and irrigation meter
readers and field inspectors. It is for DIP staff only: a phone must be registered by a DIP
supervisor before it can be used.

Meter reading
• See the meters still to read in your zones, in route order.
• Record each meter in a few steps: its condition, a clear photo, the number, and the tenant you
  checked on site.
• Warnings when a number looks too high or too low, before you save.
• Works without signal: readings and photos wait on the phone and are sent when signal is back.
• My readings and a summary of the day's work.

Field inspection
• Today's and late inspection visits from the plan.
• For each unit: as recorded, vacant, subleased, disputed, rejected or pending, with photos and notes.
• The place of the visit is kept with it, so supervisors can see it was done on site.
• Check everything on one screen and send the whole visit at once.

Made for work outside
• Large buttons and text, one main button on each screen.
• Every screen can be read aloud.
• Data on the phone is encrypted and the app can ask for the phone's lock.

Requires a DIP account and a registration code from your supervisor.
